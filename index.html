01 — how i work

Start with the user. Work outward.

The same loop whether it's an onboarding email, a website or a sales conversation.

01

Listen

Talk to the people using the product — support threads, calls, events, in‑product signals. Find the problem they actually came with.

02

Shape

Think like a product person: what needs to change in the message, the journey or the product itself so that problem gets solved?

03

Carry it through

Take it across the funnel — lifecycle, content, web, campaigns — and back to the teams who need to hear it.
