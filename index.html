01 · how i work

Start with the user. Work outward.

The same loop every time, whether it ends up as an onboarding email, a web page or a sales deck.

01

Listen

Talk to the people using the product. Support threads, onboarding calls, events, in‑product signals. Find the problem they actually came with.

at dataikuAnswering setup questions myself is where most of our use cases and success stories came from.
02

Shape

Think like a product person. What needs to change in the message, the journey or the product itself so that problem gets solved?

at dataikuNew users and advanced champions had different problems, so they got two different journeys.
03

Carry it through

Take it across the funnel: lifecycle, content, web, campaigns, sales material. Then bring it back to the teams who need to hear it.

at nautilidaThe questions buyers kept asking shaped the website, the comparison pages and the sales deck.
and back to listening
